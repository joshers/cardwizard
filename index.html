"Paying only the minimum" assumes you keep paying today's minimum every month. Real minimums usually shrink as the balance drops, which makes payoff take even longer.

Things to check before you transfer:
  • You usually can't move a balance between two cards from the same bank.
  • The promo clock often starts when the account opens, not when the transfer posts. Some offers require the transfer within 60–120 days.
  • The fee is added to the new balance and counts against the credit limit.
  • Store cards with "deferred interest" are different: if anything is left at the end, they can charge all of the interest back to day one.
  • New purchases on the transfer card may be charged the regular APR. Keep that card for the transfer only.
